Dear Kimberly Seay: 
The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) has reviewed the promotional communication, 
a direct-to-consumer (DTC) broadcast advertisement “Can’t Wait To Say ‘I’m Back’ 60” (USBK-2400890) (TV ad) for BIMZELX® (bimekizumab-bkzx) injection, for subcutaneous use 
(Bimzelx) submitted by UCB, Inc. (UCB) under cover of Form FDA 2253. FDA has 
determined that the TV ad is false or misleading. Thus the TV ad misbrands Bimzelx and 
makes the distribution of the drug in violation of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act 
(FD&C Act). 
The TV ad includes the following claims and presentations (emphasis original): 
x “I’ve always known how to fix things. … But with psoriatic arthritis, … My body felt 
broken. … Mornings were a nightmare. … But it’s not going to control me anymore.” 
(VO, frames 1-5) 
x “With BIMZELX… I can’t wait to say, … I’m Back.” (VO, frames 6-8) 
x “BIMZELX can deliver transformative relief of joint pain, and stiffness.” (VO, frame 13) 
x “Bimzelx (logo) CAN DELIVER TRANSFORMATIVE RELIEF” (on-screen text, frame 
13) 
x “I’m back to what moves me. … I’m back to reaching for new heights.” (VO, frame 15-
16) 
x “I’m back to what inspires me.” (VO, frame 20) 
x “Give yourself a chance to say, ‘I’m Back.’” (VO, frame 28) 
x “I’m Back” (on-screen text, frames 8, 9, 11, and 28) 
The totality of these claims and representations creates a misleading impression regarding 
the benefits of treatment with Bimzelx. First, the magnitude of change suggested by these 
claims implies a greater improvement than was demonstrated in the clinical trials for Bimzelx. 
Before Bimzelx treatment, the patients describe that their “body felt broken” and “mornings 
were a nightmare,” but after treatment they were “back to reaching for new heights.” 
According to the CLINICAL STUDIES section of the Prescribing Information (PI), the primary 
endpoint was the proportion of patients who achieved ACR50 response at Week 16, which
